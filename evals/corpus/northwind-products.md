# Northwind Robotics Product Overview

## Atlas picker

Atlas is Northwind's flagship picking robot. It lifts items of up to 15 kg and
picks around 600 items per hour. Atlas uses the Vision-3 camera module for item
detection and runs on the Helm operating system.

## Courier

Courier is an autonomous mobile robot that moves totes between storage and packing
stations. Courier has a top speed of 2 metres per second and a battery that lasts
10 hours. Courier depends on the Beacon positioning system, which uses ceiling
markers to localise the robot.

## Helm operating system

Helm is Northwind's robot operating system, written in Rust. Helm schedules tasks
across a fleet and exposes a REST API for warehouse management systems. Helm was
created by Northwind's platform team in 2021.

## Support

Customers get 24/7 support with a 4-hour response time for critical incidents.
Firmware updates for Atlas and Courier are released every six weeks.
