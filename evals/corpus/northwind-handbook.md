# Northwind Robotics Employee Handbook

Northwind Robotics is a fictional company used as sample data for the platform's
retrieval tests. It builds warehouse robots and is headquartered in Lisbon.

## Leave policy

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid vacation per calendar year. Up to 5
unused days can be carried over into the first quarter of the next year. Vacation
requests must be approved by the employee's team lead at least two weeks in advance.

Parental leave is 20 weeks at full pay for all parents, and can be taken in up to
three blocks during the child's first two years.

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. The Lisbon office is open
on weekdays from 8:00 to 20:00. Employees who live more than 100 km from an office
can apply to work fully remotely; applications are reviewed by the People team.

## Equipment

Every new employee receives a laptop on their first day. Engineers may choose
between a MacBook Pro and a ThinkPad X1. Lost or damaged equipment must be
reported to the IT helpdesk within 48 hours.
